# Yesim OneBalance

OneBalance links a company account with employees' Yesim accounts. The product page describes topping up a shared company balance, allocating funds to individual users and tracking usage in a management platform.

This is a corporate management offering, separate from a consumer data package. Confirm current permissions, billing, invoices, reporting, onboarding and applicable agreement with the business team. No universal savings, entitlement or service-level guarantee is established here.
