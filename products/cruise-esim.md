# Yesim Cruise eSIM guidance

Yesim's cruise page routes travellers to existing data packages for their itinerary, including global options and Pay & Fly. It focuses on ports and coastal areas where partner mobile networks have coverage.

The page does not guarantee connection quality beyond the coastal zone. Do not describe ordinary eSIM coverage as a satellite, open-ocean or ship-wide maritime service. It complements onboard connectivity rather than establishing a replacement for it.

Before choosing a package, list every port/country and check the selected destinations, allowance, validity, device support and billing unit. Do not infer complete coverage from a cruise-line name or guarantee lower costs than every onboard Wi-Fi offer.
