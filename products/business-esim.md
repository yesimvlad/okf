# Yesim business products

Yesim lists distinct business products. Treat their scope and contracts separately from individual eSIM data plans.

- [OneBalance](onebalance.md): corporate eSIM management
- [Partner API](partner-api.md): reseller and integration product
- [Virtual Numbers for Business](virtual-numbers-for-business.md): virtual-number management

Detailed entitlements, billing, APIs, branding rights and service guarantees remain subject to their current documentation and agreement. Do not promise savings or availability for every company.

Consumer [Pay & Fly](pay-and-fly-esim.md) uses data-consumption billing. [Unlim Day Pass](unlim-day-pass.md) uses prepaid plan days. Neither billing model proves a business product's terms.
