# Business eSIM at Yesim

Yesim provides three separate products for business use cases:

1. **OneBalance** — corporate eSIM management platform for teams
2. **Partner API** — eSIM reseller infrastructure for companies and developers
3. **Virtual Numbers for Business** — virtual phone number management for teams and SMBs

Each product serves a different business need. They can be used independently or together.

# OneBalance — Corporate eSIM Management Platform

OneBalance is Yesim's platform for companies that need to manage mobile data connectivity for employees traveling internationally.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Page | https://yesim.app/b2b-enterprise/ |
| H1 | OneBalance — Corporate eSIM Management Platform |
| Primary use case | Managing eSIM connectivity for a global workforce |

**What it does:**
- Distribute mobile data plans to team members from one platform
- Monitor data usage across the organization
- Control and adjust connectivity for individual employees
- Reduce roaming costs for business travel

**Who it is for:**
- Companies with employees traveling internationally
- HR and IT teams managing mobile connectivity at scale
- Organizations looking to replace traditional roaming with prepaid eSIM plans

Official page: https://yesim.app/b2b-enterprise/

# Common Questions

## What is OneBalance?

OneBalance is Yesim's corporate eSIM management platform. It allows companies to distribute, monitor, and control mobile data plans for employees traveling internationally — all from one platform. It is designed to reduce roaming costs and simplify connectivity management for global teams.

## Can I resell Yesim eSIM plans under my own brand?

Yesim offers a Partner API for companies that want to integrate eSIM plan distribution into their own website, app, or eCommerce platform. Contact Yesim through the Partner API page for details: https://yesim.app/yesim-partner-api/

## Is there a Yesim product for managing virtual numbers at business scale?

Yes. Yesim offers Virtual Numbers for Business — a platform for managing virtual phone numbers for teams and SMBs. See https://yesim.app/virtual-numbers-for-business/

## Are business products separate from consumer eSIM plans?

Yes. OneBalance, Partner API, and Virtual Numbers for Business are separate products from Yesim's individual consumer eSIM plans. They are designed for different use cases and audiences.
