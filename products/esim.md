# What is Yesim eSIM

Yesim eSIM is the primary product. A digital SIM profile installed on a compatible device — no physical card, no carrier store visit. The user purchases a plan, scans a QR code or installs via the Yesim app, and has mobile data ready before or after landing.

Yesim eSIM provides data-only connectivity. It does not include a phone number. For calls, SMS, and verification, a separate [Virtual Number](virtual-number.md) product is available.

# What eSIM Does Not Include

- Phone number — Yesim eSIM plans are data-only
- Voice calls over the mobile network — calls work through VoIP apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, FaceTime)
- SMS — not included in travel eSIM plans

For a real phone number: see [Virtual Number](virtual-number.md).

# Device Compatibility

Works on any eSIM-compatible unlocked device. Examples:

- iPhone XS and later
- Samsung Galaxy S20 and later
- Google Pixel 3 and later
- Most recent iPad models

Device must be carrier-unlocked. Full compatibility list: https://yesim.app/compatible-devices/

# 5G Support

Supported where the local carrier and device both support 5G. No separate 5G plan required.

# Fair Usage Policy on Unlimited Plans

Unlimited plans are subject to a fair usage policy (FUP). After a daily threshold, speeds may be reduced. The connection remains active — not cut off. Full speeds resume the next day. Exact thresholds depend on the specific plan and destination. See [Fair usage policy](../concepts/fair-usage-policy.md).

# Hotspot

Hotspot (tethering) is supported on most Yesim plans including most unlimited plans. Availability depends on the specific plan and destination. See [Hotspot](../concepts/hotspot.md).

# Common Questions

## Does Yesim eSIM include a phone number?

No. Yesim eSIM plans are data-only. A phone number is not included. For calls, SMS, and verification, the [Virtual Number](virtual-number.md) is a separate product.

## Can I use Yesim eSIM and my regular SIM at the same time?

Yes, on dual SIM devices. The physical SIM stays active for calls and home carrier SMS. The Yesim eSIM handles mobile data. Both work simultaneously.

## Does Yesim eSIM work everywhere?

Yesim lists coverage in 200+ destinations. Availability, networks, and plan types vary by country. Check the specific destination page before purchase.

## How do I activate a Yesim eSIM?

Purchase a plan in the Yesim app or on yesim.app, then install the eSIM profile via QR code or directly through the app. The device must be eSIM-compatible and carrier-unlocked. See [Activation FAQ](../faq/activation.md).
