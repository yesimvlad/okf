## Audience and scenarios

English-language content can address readers in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and elsewhere, without assuming their residence or purchase eligibility. Separate outbound-travel intent from visitors searching for a plan for one of those destinations.

| Scenario | Information to collect | Content direction | Conditions to retain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short single-country holiday | Destination, trip length, data demand and exact phone | Country-plan selection and installation | Current plan validity and allowance |
| Several countries in one trip | Full route and travel dates | Regional/global coverage comparison | Check each country in the selected product |
| Repeat trips | Data estimates and actual travel days | Compare Pay & Fly, day packs and fixed packages | Distinct billing units and expiry rules |
| Work abroad | Calls, uploads, streaming and tethered devices | Data estimation and hotspot conditions | No guaranteed minimum speed or uptime |
| Student travel | Stay duration, destination, device and expected use | Explain travel data and compare appropriate packages | Do not describe a supplementary travel product as permanent domestic telecom service |
| Verification enquiry | Number service and the platform’s rules | Separate number FAQ | No guaranteed OTP delivery, acceptance or account recovery |

Scenario choices are editorial hypotheses, not evidence of dominant traffic or conversion segments.

## Terminology and article rules

Use eSIM profile, data plan, installation, activation, validity, Pay & Fly, Unlim Day Pass and Virtual Number consistently. US cellular/mobile and UK mobile wording can be researched separately without inventing separate product rules. Use plain English, short answer blocks and the necessary exception in the same paragraph.

Do not impose a universal article length, anchor quota or PL/RO-only forbidden heading. Link general Yesim/eSIM context to the English home, device checks to compatibility, destination phrases to verified source routes and product comparisons to the exact product record. Keep promo codes and prices live rather than duplicating them in permanent articles.

## Comparison rules

Airalo, Holafly, Nomad and Saily are research candidates, not a confirmed local market-share ranking. Collect current official prices, coverage, allowances, validity, activation, FUP, hotspot and refund conditions for the same scenario. Do not claim Yesim is universally cheaper, faster or the only provider with a feature.
