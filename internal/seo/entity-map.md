# Internal Linking Rules

**From blog posts to product pages:**
- Country destination articles → link to the relevant `/country/{slug}/` page
- "What is eSIM" articles → link to `/what-is-esim/`
- Unlimited data articles → link to `/pay-and-fly-esim/` or `/unlim-day-pass/`
- Virtual number articles → link to `/virtual-number/` or service-specific page

**From country pages to regional pages:**
- Country pages in Europe → link to `/regions/europe-esim/`
- Country pages in Asia → link to `/regions/asia-pacific-esim/` or `/regions/south-east-asia-esim/`

**Anchor rules:**
- Use descriptive anchors — no "click here", "learn more", "this page"
- Match anchor language to the page language
- Maximum 3–4 internal links per article
- One anchor per unique destination URL per article
