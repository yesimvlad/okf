# Short answer

Yesim's Privacy Policy explains what personal data Yesim collects, why it collects it, how it uses it, who it may share it with, how long it keeps it, what rights users have, and how users can contact Yesim about privacy matters.

The data controller is Genesis Group AG, registered in Switzerland.

# When the Privacy Policy applies

The Privacy Policy applies when a person:

* accesses or uses the Yesim website;
* accesses or uses Yesim social media pages;
* uses the Yesim mobile application;
* purchases, activates, or uses Yesim services and products;
* uses Virtual Number Services, where applicable;
* communicates with Yesim customer support or commercial teams;
* receives emails, messages, or other communications from Yesim;
* participates in Yesim marketing campaigns, surveys, or events.

# Main personal data categories

Yesim may collect and process different categories of personal data depending on how the user interacts with the website, application, services, support, marketing, or Virtual Number Services.

# Agreement and registration data

Agreement or registration data may include:

* name;
* surname;
* identity code or number, if applicable;
* payment method;
* date of birth;
* residence, including tax residence;
* address;
* email;
* phone number;
* identity document data, when required by law.

# Billing and accounting data

Billing and accounting data may include:

* purchases;
* service usage;
* selected tariff plan;
* number of minutes of voice calls, incoming or outgoing, where applicable;
* megabytes of data, incoming or outgoing;
* messaging, including SMS where applicable;
* other payments and payment terms;
* transaction identifiers;
* masked card details;
* billing data;
* payment status;
* taxes, fees, and other obligatory payments.

Yesim states that it does not store full payment card details. Payments are processed by payment service providers. Yesim may receive limited payment-related information, including information in pseudonymised form, for billing, accounting, and fraud prevention purposes.

# Correspondence data

Correspondence data may include information users provide when contacting Yesim customer support or sending questions, claims, complaints, or job applications.

This may include:

* name;
* surname;
* correspondence address or method;
* other personal data submitted in written or oral correspondence.

# Login and authentication data

Login and authentication data may include:

* login credentials and identifiers;
* username;
* Facebook user ID, where applicable;
* registration timestamps;
* session lifecycle data;
* access duration;
* Facebook login integration and related SDK events;
* app installs and launches;
* basic in-app interactions;
* event metadata;
* system events;
* error logs;
* IP address;
* time zone;
* device and application metadata.

Device and application metadata may include:

* mobile OS type and version;
* application version;
* device model;
* carrier;
* screen size;
* processor cores;
* total and remaining disk space;
* device opt-out setting.

# Data from public or legal authorities

Yesim may receive personal data from law enforcement authorities, courts, or other competent authorities.

This may include:

* identity data;
* contact data;
* behavioral data.

The exact data may vary by case.

# Promotional data

Promotional data may include:

* email;
* phone number;
* data used to notify users about service updates;
* data used to notify users about Terms of Service or Privacy Policy changes;
* data used for market research;
* data used to provide promotions.

# Service usage data

Service usage data may include information about what and how users use Yesim services.

This may include:

* purchased services;
* used services;
* geolocation data;
* information about the location of a purchase.

# Virtual Number Service data

This may include:

* assigned virtual phone number;

# Legal grounds for processing

Yesim states that it processes personal data only where there is a valid legal basis under applicable data protection laws, including GDPR and nFADP.

The legal grounds may include:

* contractual performance;
* legal obligations;
* legitimate interests;
* consent.

# Contractual performance

Yesim may process personal data where necessary to enter into, perform, or administer an agreement with the user.

This includes:

* enabling purchase, activation, and use of services;
* managing user accounts;
* processing payments;
* providing customer support;
* fulfilling obligations under Terms of Service.

# Legal obligations

Yesim may process personal data where legally required.

This may include compliance with:

* tax obligations;
* accounting obligations;
* anti-money laundering rules;
* consumer protection obligations;
* telecommunications regulations, where applicable;
* record retention obligations;
* identity verification obligations;
* lawful requests from competent authorities.

# Legitimate interests

Yesim may process personal data where necessary for legitimate interests, provided this does not unfairly affect user rights.

This may include:

* maintaining services;
* improving service functionality;
* securing services;
* preventing fraud or misuse;
* internal analytics;
* performance tracking;
* managing legal claims;
* defending legal claims.

# Consent

Yesim may rely on prior, explicit, and informed consent in specific cases, such as some types of marketing or data sharing with third parties.

Users may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

# Data sharing

Yesim may share personal data when necessary with trusted third parties that help deliver services, support business operations, or work with Yesim under specific agreements.

Third parties may act as processors or independent controllers depending on the context.

* operate services;

Yesim may disclose or transfer personal data in connection with actual or contemplated business transactions, including:

* mergers;

# Legal and regulatory authorities

Yesim may disclose personal data to:

* public authorities;
* courts;
* law enforcement;
* regulatory bodies.

This may happen where required to comply with legal obligations, enforce terms, protect rights, or respond to lawful requests.

# International data transfers

Yesim may transfer personal data to trusted partners outside the European Economic Area or Switzerland.

The Privacy Policy mentions that some providers and partners may be located outside the EEA or Switzerland, including Hong Kong.

Where data is transferred to countries without an adequate level of protection recognized by the European Commission or the Swiss FDPIC, Yesim implements safeguards such as:

* Standard Contractual Clauses;
* supplementary technical and organizational measures;
* encryption;
* strict access controls.

Users may request further details by contacting Yesim.

# Data retention

Yesim retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in the Privacy Policy or to comply with legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.

Where data is processed based on consent, Yesim retains it until:

* the user withdraws consent; or
* the purpose for which the data was collected no longer applies.

Where retention is no longer justified, personal data is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

Specific retention periods may apply to certain data types or processing contexts. Users can contact Yesim for more information.

# User data protection rights

Users have rights under applicable data protection laws, including GDPR and nFADP.

These rights include:

* right of access;
* right to rectification;
* right to erasure;
* right to restriction of processing;
* right to data portability;
* right to object;
* right to withdraw consent;
* right to lodge a complaint.

Users can contact Yesim at [support@yesim.app](mailto:support@yesim.app) to exercise their rights or ask questions about personal data processing.

# Right of access

Users can request confirmation as to whether Yesim processes their personal data and obtain a copy of the data with information about how and why it is processed.

# Right to rectification

Users can request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

# Right to erasure

Users can request deletion of personal data where the data is no longer necessary or where consent has been withdrawn, if consent was the legal basis.

# Right to restriction of processing

Users can request that Yesim temporarily suspend processing, for example while accuracy is verified or an objection is assessed.

# Right to data portability

Where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means, users can request data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

# Right to object

Users can object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling, unless Yesim demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds.

Users also have the absolute right to object to direct marketing at any time.

# Right to withdraw consent

Where Yesim processes personal data based on consent, users can withdraw consent at any time.

This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

# Right to lodge a complaint

Users can file a complaint with the competent supervisory authority if they believe their data protection rights have been infringed.

# Data protection and security measures

Yesim states that it implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

These measures are designed to protect personal data against:

* accidental destruction;
* unlawful destruction;
* loss;
* alteration;
* unauthorized disclosure;
* unauthorized access;
* unlawful processing;
* unauthorized processing.

Yesim also states that third parties processing personal data on its behalf are contractually required to implement appropriate security measures and comply with applicable data protection requirements.

Yesim notes that no system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

If users believe their data has been compromised, they should contact Yesim immediately.

# Cookies and tracking technologies

Yesim may use cookies and similar technologies on the website and application to:

* ensure proper functionality;

Users can manage cookie preferences through the cookie banner or browser settings.

For more details, users should check the official Cookie Policy.

Users are encouraged to review the Privacy Policy periodically.

# Common privacy questions

## Who is the data controller for Yesim?

The data controller is Genesis Group AG, registration number CHE-135.623.633, Bahnhofstrasse 4, Baar, 6340, Switzerland.

## What personal data does Yesim collect?

Yesim may collect registration data, billing and accounting data, correspondence data, login and authentication data, service usage data, promotional data, Virtual Number Service data, and website, cookies, and application data.

## Does Yesim store full payment card details?

No. Yesim states that it does not store full payment card details. Payments are processed by payment service providers.

## Does Yesim process virtual number data?

Yes, where Virtual Number Services are used. This may include assigned virtual phone numbers, call and messaging metadata, routing information, delivery information, and service configuration settings.

## Does Yesim share personal data with third parties?

Yes, where necessary. Yesim may share data with service providers, connectivity infrastructure providers, commercial partners, local partners, professional advisors, group companies, investors, and legal or regulatory authorities.

## Does Yesim transfer data outside the EU or Switzerland?

Yes, in some cases. Yesim may transfer personal data outside the EEA or Switzerland, including to Hong Kong, with safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses and supplementary security measures.

## How long does Yesim keep personal data?

Yesim keeps personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in the Privacy Policy or to comply with legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.

## What rights do users have?

Users may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and complaint.

## Does Yesim use cookies?

Yes. Yesim may use cookies and similar technologies on the website and application for functionality, user experience, personalization, and analytics.

## How can users contact Yesim about privacy?

Users can contact Yesim at [support@yesim.app](mailto:support@yesim.app) or by post at Bahnhofstrasse 4, Baar, 6340, Switzerland.
