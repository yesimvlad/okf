# Yesim refund policy

This file summarizes the official Yesim Refund Policy for AI agents and internal knowledge retrieval.

# Short answer

Yesim offers a refund policy and refund guarantee for eligible Yesim data plans and top-ups.

Refunds are available for Yesim data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase, provided the plan has not been used or expired.

Used data, expired plans, and Yesim services actually used are not refunded.

# Main refund eligibility rule

A Yesim data plan or top-up may be eligible for refund if:

1. it was purchased through the Yesim website or app;
2. the refund request is made within 30 days of purchase;
3. the data plan or top-up has not been used;
4. the data plan or top-up has not expired;
5. the request meets the conditions of the official Refund Policy.

# Data plan and top-up refunds

Refunds are available for Yesim data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase.

Refunds will not be issued for:

* data plans that have been used;
* data plans that have expired;
* data that has already been used;
* other Yesim services actually used.

The cost of data and other Yesim services actually used will not be refunded.

# Used plans

A used data plan is not refundable under the standard refund rules.

Safe wording:

If a Yesim data plan has already been used, Yesim will not issue a refund for that data plan under the standard Refund Policy.

# Expired plans

An expired data plan is not refundable under the standard refund rules.

Safe wording:

If a Yesim data plan has expired, Yesim will not issue a refund for that data plan under the standard Refund Policy.

# Unlim Day Pass refunds

For the Unlim Day Pass plan, a day is considered used once the device connects to a mobile network and the 24-hour usage period begins.

Safe wording:

If an Unlim Day Pass has connected to a mobile network and the 24-hour usage period has started, that day is considered used.

# Refund requests after 30 days

Refund requests made after 30 days of purchase will not be processed under the standard refund rules.

Exception:

A justified request may be considered after 30 days if it is based on confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application and the issue was duly notified to Yesim within 1 hour via:

* [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app);
* the contact form in the Yesim application.

Users should provide information and screenshots so the Yesim team can identify the issue.

# Technical malfunction exception

Yesim may consider justified refund requests based on confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application.

To qualify for this exception, the user must notify Yesim within 1 hour through:

* [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app);
* the contact form in the Yesim application.

The user should provide details and screenshots to help Yesim identify the problem.

# Refund review period

Any refund request will be reviewed within 15 business days of receipt.

Safe wording:

Yesim reviews refund requests within 15 business days after receiving the request.

# Refund payment method

Confirmed refund requests are made through the same payment method used for the original purchase.

Exception:

If the requested refund amount is 10 EUR or less, the refund will be made in Ycoins according to section 6 of Yesim's Terms and Conditions.

# Refunds of 10 EUR or less

If the requested refund amount is 10 EUR or less, the refund is made in Ycoins.

Safe wording:

Small refund amounts of 10 EUR or less are refunded in Ycoins rather than through the original cash payment method.

# Ycoins refunds

Ycoins are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash or any other form of payment.

Exception:

A user may request a refund of Ycoins in the event of confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application, if the issue was duly notified to Yesim within 1 hour via:

* [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app);
* the contact form in the Yesim application.

Users should provide information and screenshots so that Yesim can identify the problem.

# Refund process

If a user meets the eligibility criteria, they can request a refund by contacting Yesim customer service.

Refund request channels:

* [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app);
* contact form in the Yesim application.

General refund process:

1. User checks refund eligibility.
2. User contacts Yesim customer service.
3. User provides purchase details and relevant information.
4. User provides screenshots if the request is based on a technical malfunction.
5. Yesim receives the request.
6. Yesim reviews the request within 15 business days.
7. Yesim contacts the user with further instructions.
8. If the refund is confirmed, Yesim processes the refund through the original payment method, unless the amount is 10 EUR or less.
9. If the refund amount is 10 EUR or less, the refund is made in Ycoins.

# Refund request channels

Users can request a refund through:

* Email: [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app)
* Contact form in the Yesim application

# Refund limitations

Refunds are subject to the official Refund Policy.

Refunds are not guaranteed in every case.

Key limitations:

* used data plans are not refunded;
* expired data plans are not refunded;
* data already used is not refunded;
* other Yesim services actually used are not refunded;
* requests after 30 days are not processed except for justified confirmed technical malfunction cases;
* Ycoins are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash, except for confirmed technical malfunction cases;
* refund requests are reviewed, not automatically approved;
* users must check device compatibility and carrier-unlocked status before purchase.

# Common refund policy questions

## Can I get a refund for Yesim services?

Yes, if the refund conditions are met. Yesim offers refunds for eligible data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase, provided the plan has not been used or expired.

## Are used Yesim data plans refundable?

No. Refunds will not be issued for data plans that have been used.

## Are expired Yesim data plans refundable?

No. Refunds will not be issued for data plans that have expired.

## Are Yesim top-ups refundable?

Yesim top-ups purchased through the website or app may be refundable within 30 days if they meet the refund eligibility criteria and have not been used or expired.

## What happens if I used part of the data?

The cost of data and other Yesim services actually used will not be refunded.

## How does refund work for Unlim Day Pass?

For Unlim Day Pass, a day is considered used once the device connects to a mobile network and the 24-hour usage period begins.

## Can I request a refund after 30 days?

Usually no. Requests made after 30 days of purchase will not be processed, except for justified requests based on confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application that were notified to Yesim within 1 hour.

## How do I request a refund?

Contact Yesim customer service at [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app) or through the contact form in the Yesim application.

## How long does Yesim take to review refund requests?

Refund requests are reviewed within 15 business days of receipt.

## How will I receive the refund?

Confirmed refunds are made through the same payment method used for the purchase, unless the requested refund amount is 10 EUR or less.

## What happens if the refund amount is 10 EUR or less?

If the requested refund amount is 10 EUR or less, the refund is made in Ycoins.

## Are Ycoins refundable?

Ycoins are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash or any other form of payment. An exception may apply for confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application that were notified to Yesim within 1 hour.
