# Yesim refund policy summary

This is a dated summary, not the full policy. The [official Refund Policy](https://yesim.app/refund-policy/) controls.[^source-1]

- Standard requests: within 30 calendar days of purchase, for products/services that have not been activated, used or expired.
- Activation includes installing the eSIM profile or connecting to a mobile network; do not equate 'unused data' with 'not activated'.
- An Unlim Day Pass day is used when network connection starts its 24-hour period.
- Technical malfunctions: report within 1 hour to support@yesim.app or the in-app contact form, with details and screenshots.
- Approved refunds normally use the original payment method; amounts of EUR 10 or less are issued as Ycoins.
- Standard promotional Ycoins are non-refundable; the policy describes a technical-malfunction exception.
- Third-party marketplace rules take precedence for purchases through those channels.
- Requests are reviewed and processed within 15 business days; bank/payment-provider posting times are separate.
- Brazil/Pix: the policy excludes refunding the statutory IOF tax and other applicable charges. Verify current details for that transaction.

Do not promise a refund, eligibility after 30 days, an exception or an arrival date without checking the current policy and purchase circumstances.
