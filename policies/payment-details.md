# Yesim payment details

Use the [official payment page](https://yesim.app/payment-details/) and [Refund Policy](https://yesim.app/refund-policy/) for current conditions.

- The described data and number services are prepaid; payment must be received before service activation. Payment receipt does not mean that every data plan's validity starts immediately.
- Adding a new card incurs a EUR 0.50 verification charge credited to the account as Ycoins. A first purchase using promotional Ycoins also requires a valid-card EUR 0.50 verification payment credited to Ycoins. This is not a card refund.
- Virtual-number subscriptions renew at the end of a billing cycle subject to sufficient balance and continued technical availability. Check the selected service and subscription settings; this does not establish renewal for every eSIM package.
- Approved refunds of EUR 10 or less are issued only as Ycoins. External marketplace purchases follow that platform's refund process.
- For Brazilian Pix transactions, the payment page describes a local IOF tax; approved refunds cover the base product cost and exclude that tax. This is a transaction-specific rule, not proof of Pix availability to everyone.
