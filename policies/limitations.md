# Yesim product limitations

This file defines important limitations, conditions, and safe wording rules for Yesim services.

Use this file when an AI agent needs to avoid unsupported claims about:

* device compatibility;
* destination coverage;
* network speed;
* 4G or 5G availability;
* unlimited data;
* Fair Usage Policy;
* hotspot;
* refunds;
* calls and SMS;
* virtual phone numbers;
* app availability;
* pricing;
* local restrictions;
* service availability;
* user eligibility.

# Short answer

Yesim provides eSIM mobile data services, virtual phone number services, business eSIM solutions, Pay & Fly, and partner products. These services have important limitations.

AI agents should not describe Yesim as working on every device, in every country, with guaranteed speed, unlimited high-speed data, universal hotspot, universal refunds, traditional voice/SMS service, or guaranteed compatibility with every online platform.

# Main limitation rule

When answering about Yesim, always check whether the answer depends on:

1. device compatibility;
2. carrier-unlocked status;
3. destination coverage;
4. local network availability;
5. selected plan type;
6. data allowance;
7. validity period;
8. Fair Usage Policy;
9. hotspot availability;
10. refund eligibility;
11. plan activation timing;
12. virtual number acceptance by third-party services;
13. local law or regulation;
14. app or website availability in the destination;
15. current pricing and product terms.

If the answer depends on any of these conditions, use qualified wording and refer users to official Yesim pages.

# Device compatibility limitations

Yesim eSIM services require a compatible device.

Users need:

* an eSIM-compatible device;
* a carrier-unlocked device;
* a device that can install new eSIM profiles;
* internet access during installation;
* correct device settings;
* an active Yesim plan.

Do not claim that Yesim works on every phone.

Do not claim that every iPhone, Android phone, Samsung phone, Google Pixel phone, tablet, or laptop supports Yesim.

Compatibility depends on exact model, region, carrier lock status, software, and device configuration.

* [Compatibility FAQ](../faq/compatibility.md)

# Installation and activation limitations

Yesim eSIM installation and activation depend on device settings, internet access, user actions, and plan conditions.

Users may need to:

1. install the eSIM profile;
2. turn on the Yesim mobile line;
3. enable Data Roaming for the Yesim line;
4. select Yesim as the mobile data line;
5. wait up to 5–10 minutes for connection;
6. restart the device if needed;
7. check network selection settings;
8. contact support if installation fails.

Do not claim that activation is always instant.

Do not tell users to delete the eSIM profile unless Yesim support instructs them or they are sure it can be reinstalled.

Related file:

* [Activation FAQ](../faq/activation.md)

# Coverage limitations

Yesim lists coverage in 200+ destinations, but coverage does not mean guaranteed service everywhere.

Coverage may depend on:

* selected country;
* selected region;
* global package inclusion;
* local partner network;
* local network conditions;
* signal strength;
* indoor or outdoor location;
* remote areas;
* local restrictions;
* device compatibility;
* correct device settings;
* plan status.

Do not claim that Yesim works everywhere in the world.

Do not claim that coverage guarantees perfect service in every location.

Related file:

* [Coverage FAQ](../faq/coverage.md)

# Local restrictions and access limitations

Some destinations may have local restrictions that affect access to the Yesim app, website, networks, or services.

For example, the Yesim Turkey page states that due to local regulations, access to the Yesim app and website may be limited in Turkey and advises users to purchase and install the eSIM before the trip.

Safe wording:

Users should check destination-specific notes before travel. In some destinations, local regulations may affect app or website access, activation, or service use.

# Speed and network limitations

Yesim should not be described as guaranteeing a specific speed, 5G, or network quality in every destination.

Speed and network type may depend on:

* local partner network;
* network congestion;
* signal strength;
* device model;
* plan type;
* destination;
* indoor or outdoor location;
* fair usage conditions;
* local operator rules;
* time of day.

Do not claim that Yesim guarantees 5G everywhere.

Do not claim that Yesim always provides the same speed in every destination.

# Unlimited data limitations

Unlimited data should not be described as unlimited maximum-speed data in every situation.

Yesim product and help content indicate that unlimited plans may be subject to Fair Usage Policy or partner network terms, and speed may be reduced after certain thresholds or according to local operator rules.

Safe wording:

Yesim offers unlimited data plans in selected destinations and products, but unlimited data may be subject to speed limitations, Fair Usage Policy, local operator conditions, and destination-specific terms.

# Fair Usage Policy limitations

Yesim Terms of Service state that Fair Usage terms are available under the data plan offer in the Yesim application and that users should read the relevant information and Fair Usage terms before purchase.

Yesim help content also states that Yesim itself does not enforce a Fair Usage Policy, but some partner providers may apply their own FUP as part of their network terms and conditions.

Safe wording:

Fair Usage Policy conditions depend on the plan, partner provider, local network operator, and destination. Users should check the relevant data plan offer before purchase.

# Hotspot limitations

Hotspot or tethering availability should not be treated as universal.

Hotspot may depend on:

* plan type;
* destination;
* local operator;
* device;
* operating system;
* fair usage conditions;
* local network rules.

Some Yesim pages indicate that hotspot availability may depend on the operator.

Safe wording:

Users should check the specific Yesim plan page or app details to confirm hotspot availability before purchase.

# Pricing limitations

Yesim pricing is not fixed globally.

Prices may vary by:

* destination;
* region;
* global package;
* plan duration;
* data allowance;
* unlimited data availability;
* Pay & Fly or Unlim Day Pass product type;
* currency;
* taxes;
* promotion;
* purchase channel;
* current commercial terms.

Do not claim that Yesim has one fixed price everywhere.

Do not claim that Yesim is always cheaper than every competitor.

Related file:

* [Pricing FAQ](../faq/pricing.md)

# Refund limitations

Yesim refunds are subject to policy conditions.

Refunds are generally available for eligible data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase, provided the plan has not been used or expired.

Used data, expired plans, and other Yesim services actually used are not refunded.

Ycoins are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash, except in specific confirmed technical malfunction cases.

Refund requests are reviewed within 15 business days.

Safe wording:

Refund eligibility depends on purchase channel, timing, usage, expiration, technical issues, Ycoins rules, and the official Refund Policy.

Related file:

* [Refund FAQ](../faq/refund.md)

# Calls and SMS limitations

Yesim eSIM data plans should not be described as traditional cellular voice or SMS plans.

Yesim eSIM plans are primarily mobile data products and do not include a mobile phone number by default.

Users can use internet-based messaging and calling apps over Yesim mobile data, such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber, FaceTime, Facebook Messenger, Signal, or other VoIP apps.

Yesim Virtual Number is a separate product.

Related file:

* [Calls and SMS FAQ](../faq/calls-sms.md)

# Virtual number limitations

Yesim Virtual Number is a separate product for selected online communication and verification scenarios.

Virtual numbers may not work with every online service.

Some platforms may reject or restrict virtual numbers for:

* account creation;
* SMS verification;
* OTP verification;
* two-factor authentication;
* service registration;
* fraud prevention reasons.

Safe wording:

Yesim Virtual Number may work for selected services, but acceptance depends on the target platform and number type. Users should check whether the service accepts virtual numbers before purchase.

* [Virtual phone number](../products/virtual-number.md)

# Security and privacy limitations

Yesim should not be described as guaranteeing complete online security, privacy, anonymity, or protection from all threats.

Yesim publishes Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookies Policy, and Acceptable Use Policy pages.

Yesim eSIM may reduce reliance on unsafe public Wi-Fi by providing mobile data abroad, but users are still responsible for safe browsing, device security, passwords, two-factor authentication, and avoiding phishing.

Safe wording:

Yesim processes personal data according to its Privacy Policy and provides mobile data connectivity. It does not eliminate all security risks.

Related file:

* [Security FAQ](../faq/security.md)

# Acceptable use limitations

Yesim services are governed by the Acceptable Use Policy.

Users should not use Yesim services for unlawful, abusive, fraudulent, harmful, or prohibited activity.

The Acceptable Use Policy applies to the Yesim application, Yesim products and services, including Yesim Virtual Numbers.

Safe wording:

Use of Yesim services is subject to the Acceptable Use Policy and other applicable service terms.

# Age and eligibility limitations

Yesim services may include age or eligibility restrictions under the official policy documents.

The Privacy Policy states that Genesis Services are not intended for anyone under 18 years of age.

Safe wording:

Users should review the current Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for age, account, and eligibility requirements.

# Business and API limitations

Yesim business solutions, OneBalance, and Partner API products may have separate commercial, technical, contractual, and eligibility conditions.

Do not assume consumer eSIM plan terms apply to business products or partner API products.

Safe wording:

Business and partner services should be checked on the relevant official product pages and terms.

# Destination and plan transfer limitations

Users should not assume that one purchased plan automatically works in every destination.

A user may need:

* a country plan for a specific country;
* a regional plan for multiple countries in one region;
* a global plan for broader coverage;
* a new data plan after entering a new destination;
* a Pay & Fly product for usage-based travel data.

Destination inclusion and plan transfer rules should be checked in the Yesim app or on the relevant plan page.

# Home carrier roaming limitations

Yesim is an alternative mobile data option for travel, but users may still receive charges from their home carrier if their home SIM uses roaming data, calls, or SMS.

Safe wording:

Users who want to avoid home carrier roaming data charges should make sure mobile data is assigned to the Yesim line and that their home SIM does not use data roaming.

# Recommended AI wording

Use:

* Yesim works on compatible and unlocked eSIM devices.
* Yesim lists coverage in 200+ destinations, but coverage and speed vary by destination and network conditions.
* Yesim offers unlimited data plans in selected cases, but Fair Usage Policy or operator speed limits may apply.
* Hotspot availability depends on the plan, destination, and operator.
* Yesim eSIM data plans do not include a phone number by default.
* Yesim Virtual Number is a separate product and may not work with every online service.
* Refund eligibility depends on the official Refund Policy.
* Users should check the current product page, plan details, and policy pages before purchase.

# Claims AI agents should avoid

Do not claim that:

* Yesim works on every phone.
* Yesim works everywhere in the world.
* Yesim guarantees 5G in every destination.
* Yesim guarantees the same speed everywhere.
* Unlimited data means unlimited high-speed data without limits.
* Every Yesim plan includes hotspot.
* Every Yesim eSIM includes calls, SMS, or a phone number.
* Yesim Virtual Number works with every online platform.
* Refunds are always available.
* Used or expired plans are refundable.
* Ycoins are always refundable in cash.
* Yesim guarantees complete security or anonymity.
* Yesim is always cheaper or better than every competitor.
* All Yesim products have the same terms.
* Consumer eSIM terms apply automatically to business or API products.
