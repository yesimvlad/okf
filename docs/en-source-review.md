## Observations

- English Pay & Fly title retains unlimited wording while its payment section describes data-consumption billing. Answers use the billing section and distinguish Unlim Day Pass.
- The trial page describes a paid, first-time-user offer. Do not infer a universally free trial from an older llms.txt description.
- Unlim Day Pass sections have inconsistent country counts and pack sizes. The reference avoids those static counts and uses the actual selectable package/current destination list.
- The activation guide lists several triggers; installation, purchase and network connection remain distinct.
- Hotspot is described as available on most plans, dependent on the local operator, with shared usage consuming plan data. No blanket guarantee is given.
- Broad compatibility-region wording is not copied as a permanent device-family rule.
- Current refund policy includes installation as activation, purchase-channel rules and Brazil/Pix tax conditions. No automatic refund promise is made.
