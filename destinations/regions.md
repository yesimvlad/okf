# Regional eSIM Plans at Yesim

Yesim regional plans cover multiple countries within one geographic area on a single eSIM. The user buys one plan and uses it across all supported countries in the region without switching plans.

Main regional plans page: https://yesim.app/regions/

# Regional vs Country vs Global Plans

| Plan type | Best for | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Regional plan | Multi-country trips within one region | One region |
| Country plan | Single-country trips | One country |
| Global plan | Multi-region or worldwide trips | 83–200+ destinations |

For country plans see [Countries](countries.md). For global plans see [Global](global.md).

# Common Questions

## Can I use a regional plan in all countries within that region?

Coverage depends on the specific plan. Not every country in a region may be included. Check the destination list on the specific regional plan page before purchase.

## Is a regional plan cheaper than buying separate country plans?

Regional plans are often more cost-effective for multi-country trips within the same region. For single-country trips, a country plan may be more affordable. Compare current prices on the relevant pages.

## Can I use a Europe eSIM in the UK?

Yes. The Yesim Europe & UK regional plan covers both EU countries and the United Kingdom on one plan.

## What is the Football Fan Plan 2026?

A special regional plan designed for travelers attending football events in 2026. Covers destinations relevant to the tournament. Check the plan page for current coverage and pricing: https://yesim.app/regions/football-fan-plan-esim/
