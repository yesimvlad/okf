# Yesim multi-destination products

Coverage belongs to the selected product or plan, not to the brand as a whole. The total number of destinations offered by Yesim must not be substituted for the coverage of an individual package.

| Option | Billing/validity context | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Pay & Fly | Charges for data used; current country rates apply | [Product](../products/pay-and-fly-esim.md) |
| Unlim Day Pass | Annual prepaid pack of 24-hour plan days | [Product](../products/unlim-day-pass.md) |
| Global packages | Selected package allowance, validity and country list | [Current global catalogue](https://yesim.app/global/) |

Before recommending a multi-country plan, check all trip countries against that plan's current list. Check networks, speed, hotspot, fair-use rules and activation terms separately. A geographic region name does not prove that every country in the region is included.

Do not infer daily unlimited billing for Pay & Fly or unconditionally non-expiring days for Unlim Day Pass.
