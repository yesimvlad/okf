# Destinations

Yesim eSIM coverage by destination type. Use these files to find available plans for a specific country, region, or global travel.

* [Countries](countries.md) — eSIM plans for 173 individual countries, grouped by region.
* [Regions](regions.md) — Regional eSIM plans covering multiple countries in one plan: Europe, Asia Pacific, Middle East, Americas, Africa, Caribbean, and more.
* [Global](global.md) — Global and multi-destination plans: Pay & Fly, Global Package, Global Plus Package, and Unlim Day Pass.
