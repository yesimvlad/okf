# Yesim privacy and account FAQ

## Does Yesim process personal data?

Yes. The Privacy Policy describes account, billing, correspondence, authentication, technical and other categories. Consult the relevant current clause for purposes, recipients, retention and rights.

## Does Yesim store no payment-related information?

The policy says full payment-card details are not stored by Yesim; payments use payment service providers. Limited information such as transaction identifiers, masked card details, billing data and payment status may be received and stored. These are different claims.

## Is a virtual number completely anonymous?

Keeping a personal number separate from some registrations does not establish anonymity to the provider or third parties. The Terms permit identity-verification requests in applicable circumstances. Third-party acceptance is not guaranteed.

## Does eSIM internet eliminate phishing or account risks?

A data connection is not a guarantee against phishing, compromised devices or account misuse. Do not convert connectivity or marketing privacy language into a security certification.

## Where should privacy requests go?

Use the current Privacy Policy's contact route and relevant rights clause. For purchase or connection issues use [support](support.md). Do not invent a deletion deadline or legal entitlement.
