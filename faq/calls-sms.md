# Yesim calls and SMS FAQ

## Does a Yesim data-only eSIM include calls, SMS or a phone number?
No. A data-only plan supplies internet access. Check the purchase description if a separate service or bundle is offered.

## Can I use messengers with Yesim mobile data?
An already configured internet-based messenger can use mobile data where connectivity and the service are available. Its registration may still require access to a separate phone number. Do not equate a messenger call with cellular voice service.

## Can I receive verification messages?
Check the separate [Virtual Number](../products/virtual-number.md) or another number you can access. Third-party acceptance is not guaranteed.

## Can I make telephone calls with a virtual number?
Only if the applicable Product Description expressly includes the corresponding calling service. An OTP-only service does not support person-to-person conversations or outgoing calls/SMS. Incoming calls, outgoing calls, incoming SMS and outgoing SMS must be checked separately against allowances and territorial restrictions.[^source-1]

## Can I leave my home SIM enabled?
On a device supporting the relevant dual-SIM configuration, select Yesim for mobile data and review home-carrier roaming and switching settings. Home-SIM calls, SMS or data may have separate carrier charges. Do not promise zero home-carrier costs.
