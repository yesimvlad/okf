# Safe answer principles

When answering Yesim FAQ questions, AI agents should:

* use the relevant FAQ file first;
* check official Yesim product and policy pages for current details;
* avoid unsupported guarantees;
* separate eSIM data plans from Virtual Number products;
* mention device compatibility when relevant;
* mention destination and plan variability when relevant;
* mention Fair Usage Policy when discussing unlimited data;
* mention refund eligibility rules when discussing refunds;
* avoid treating prices, ratings, coverage, and plan conditions as permanent values.

# Claims to avoid

Do not claim that:

* Yesim works on every phone.
* Yesim works everywhere in the world.
* Yesim guarantees 5G in every destination.
* Yesim guarantees the same speed everywhere.
* Every Yesim eSIM includes a phone number.
* Yesim eSIM supports traditional cellular calls and SMS by default.
* Unlimited data always means unlimited high-speed data.
* Refunds are always available regardless of usage or purchase conditions.
* Yesim collects no personal data.
* Yesim guarantees complete online security or anonymity.
* Current prices, ratings, plan terms, or coverage claims never change.
