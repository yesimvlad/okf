# Yesim support and troubleshooting

## Where can I get help?

Use the [Help Center](https://help.yesim.tech/) and the support/contact route in the Yesim app or [Contacts page](https://yesim.app/contacts/). The official sources list support@yesim.app for service issues and b2b@yesim.app for business enquiries.

24/7 support is advertised. An advertised average response time is not a guaranteed deadline or proof of support in every website language.

## What should I check if data is not working?

Confirm the plan includes the destination and is active with allowance remaining. Check the installed/enabled Yesim profile, mobile-data selection, data roaming and network settings using the current device instructions. Restart as advised and contact support if the issue persists.

## What information should I provide?

Explain the issue, destination, exact device/software, purchased plan and relevant settings/screenshots through the official support route. Keep credentials and installation QR codes out of public posts.

## What about a technical refund request?

The current Refund Policy requires reporting technical malfunction within one hour with details and screenshots. Follow its purchase-channel and eligibility conditions; troubleshooting does not establish a refund entitlement.
