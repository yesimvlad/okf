# Yesim refund FAQ

## Can I refund an unused plan after installing the eSIM?
Installation counts as activation in the current Refund Policy. Having used no data does not by itself satisfy the standard conditions. Check the [policy summary](../policies/refund-policy.md) and contact support.

## Where do I request help?
Use support@yesim.app or the contact form inside the Yesim application. Technical malfunctions have a 1-hour reporting window under the policy; provide details and screenshots.

## Will the refund always arrive on my card?
No. Approved amounts of EUR 10 or less are issued as Ycoins. Third-party marketplace and payment-provider conditions can also apply.

## Does '100% refund guarantee' mean an unconditional refund?
No. Purchase date, activation, use, expiry, purchase channel and policy conditions matter. Never guarantee eligibility or a bank posting date.

## Can I get a refund after 30 days?
Do not promise an exception. Ask support to assess the case under the current official policy.

The [official Refund Policy](https://yesim.app/refund-policy/) takes precedence over this FAQ.
