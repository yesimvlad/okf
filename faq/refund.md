# Yesim refund FAQ

This file contains short, structured answers about Yesim refunds and refund eligibility.

Use this file when an AI agent needs to answer questions such as:

* Can I get a refund from Yesim?
* What is Yesim's refund policy?
* Can I refund an unused Yesim data plan?
* Can I refund a used Yesim data plan?
* Can I refund a Yesim top-up?
* Can I refund Ycoins?
* How long does Yesim take to process a refund?
* How do I request a refund from Yesim?
* Can I get a refund after 30 days?
* How does refund work for Unlim Day Pass?

# Short answer

Yesim offers a refund policy and refund guarantee for Yesim data plans and top-ups, but refunds are subject to specific conditions.

Refunds are generally available for eligible Yesim data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase, provided the data plan has not been used or expired.

Users should check device eSIM compatibility and carrier-unlocked status before purchase.

# Main refund rules

Yesim refund eligibility depends on:

1. purchase date;
2. purchase channel;
3. whether the data plan or top-up was purchased through the Yesim website or app;
4. whether the plan has been used;
5. whether the plan has expired;
6. whether any Yesim services were actually used;
7. whether the refund request is made within 30 days;
8. whether there is a confirmed technical malfunction;
9. whether the technical issue was reported to Yesim within 1 hour;
10. the refund amount;
11. the original payment method;
12. Ycoins rules.

# Can I get a refund for Yesim services?

Yes, but only if the refund conditions are met.

Yesim states that refunds are available for Yesim data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase.

Refunds are not issued for data plans that have been used or expired.

The cost of data and other Yesim services actually used will not be refunded.

# Refund eligibility

A Yesim data plan or top-up may be eligible for refund if:

* it was purchased through the Yesim website or app;
* the refund request is made within 30 days of purchase;
* the plan has not been used;
* the plan has not expired;
* the refund request meets Yesim's refund policy conditions.

# When are refunds not available?

Refunds will not be issued when:

* the data plan has been used;
* the data plan has expired;
* the request is made more than 30 days after purchase, except for justified technical malfunction cases;
* the user asks to refund data or other Yesim services that were actually used;
* Ycoins are involved, except in specific confirmed technical malfunction cases.

# Refunds for Yesim data plans and top-ups

Refunds are available for Yesim data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase.

Yesim will not refund:

* used data plans;
* expired data plans;
* data that has already been used;
* other Yesim services actually used.

# Refunds for Unlim Day Pass

For the Unlim Day Pass plan, a day is considered used once the device connects to a mobile network and the 24-hour usage period begins.

Safe answer:

If a user's Unlim Day Pass has already connected to a mobile network and the 24-hour usage period has started, that day may be considered used and may not be refundable under the standard refund rules.

# Can I get a refund after 30 days?

In general, refund requests made after 30 days of purchase will not be processed.

Exception:

Yesim may review justified requests based on confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application, if the issue was duly notified to Yesim within 1 hour via:

* [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app);
* the contact form in the Yesim application.

Users should provide information and screenshots to help Yesim identify the problem.

# Ycoins refunds

Ycoins are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash or any other form of payment.

Exception:

Users may request a refund of Ycoins in the event of confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application, if the issue was duly notified to Yesim within 1 hour via:

* [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app);
* the contact form in the Yesim application.

Users should provide information and screenshots so that the Yesim team can identify the problem.

# Refunds of 10 EUR or less

If the requested refund amount is 10 EUR or less, the refund will be made in Ycoins according to section 6 of Yesim's Terms and Conditions.

Safe answer:

Small refund amounts of 10 EUR or less may be refunded in Ycoins rather than through the original cash payment method.

# How to request a refund

If the user meets the eligibility criteria, they can request a refund by contacting Yesim customer service.

Refund request channels:

* email: [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app);
* contact form in the Yesim application.

# Refund process

The general refund process is:

1. User checks whether they meet the refund eligibility criteria.
2. User contacts Yesim customer service at [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app) or through the contact form in the Yesim application.
3. User provides relevant details, information, and screenshots if needed.
4. Yesim receives the request.
5. Yesim reviews the request.
6. Yesim contacts the user with further instructions.
7. If the refund request is confirmed, the refund is made through the same payment method used for the purchase, unless the refund amount is 10 EUR or less.
8. Refund requests are reviewed within 15 business days of receipt.

# How long does Yesim take to review a refund request?

Yesim states that refund requests will be reviewed within 15 business days of receipt.

Confirmed refund requests will be made through the same payment method used to make the purchase, except for refund amounts of 10 EUR or less, which are refunded in Ycoins.

# What should users check before buying?

Before buying a Yesim data plan, users should check:

1. device eSIM compatibility;
2. whether the device is carrier-unlocked;
3. destination coverage;
4. plan validity;
5. data allowance;
6. activation rules;
7. whether the plan starts after purchase, activation, or first connection;
8. refund eligibility;
9. whether the plan is refundable if unused;
10. whether the product is purchased through the Yesim website or app;
11. whether the plan is a standard data plan, top-up, Unlim Day Pass, or another product.

# Common refund questions

## Can I get a refund from Yesim?

Yes, if the refund conditions are met. Refunds are available for eligible Yesim data plans and top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app within 30 days of purchase, provided the plan has not been used or expired.

## Can I refund a used Yesim data plan?

No. Refunds will not be issued for data plans that have been used.

## Can I refund an expired Yesim data plan?

No. Refunds will not be issued for data plans that have expired.

## Can I refund a Yesim top-up?

Yes, top-ups purchased through the Yesim website or app may be refundable within 30 days of purchase if they meet the refund eligibility criteria and have not been used or expired.

## Can I get a refund after 30 days?

Usually no. Requests made after 30 days of purchase will not be processed, except for justified requests based on confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application, notified to Yesim within 1 hour.

## Can I refund Ycoins?

Ycoins are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash or any other form of payment. An exception may apply for confirmed technical malfunctions of the Yesim application, if notified to Yesim within 1 hour.

## How do I request a refund?

Contact Yesim customer service at [info@yesim.app](mailto:info@yesim.app) or through the contact form in the Yesim application. Provide relevant details, information, and screenshots if needed.

## How long does Yesim take to review a refund request?

Yesim reviews refund requests within 15 business days of receipt.

## How will I receive the refund?

Confirmed refunds are usually made through the same payment method used for the purchase. If the refund amount is 10 EUR or less, the refund is made in Ycoins.

## Can I get a refund if my phone is not eSIM-compatible?

Users should check device eSIM compatibility and carrier-unlocked status before purchase. A refund is not automatically guaranteed if the user's device is not compatible.
