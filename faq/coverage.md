# Yesim coverage and network FAQ

## Where does Yesim work?

Use the selected product's current destination list. The [country catalogue](../destinations/countries.md), [regional catalogue](../destinations/regions.md) and [global catalogue](../destinations/global.md) are source routes. A brand-wide destination total is not the coverage of every package.

## Will a regional or global plan cover my whole itinerary?

Check every country against that exact package, including transit stops where data is needed. A region name or an available country page does not prove inclusion in another package.

## Which operator will the phone use?

Partner networks depend on destination and selected service. Confirm current operators on the plan or with support. Do not assume one operator worldwide.

## Is 5G or a minimum speed guaranteed?

No universal guarantee is established. Device support, local operator availability, location, signal and selected conditions matter. Coverage, speed and data allowance are different properties.

## Does unlimited data remove network restrictions?

Check the applicable [fair-use conditions](../concepts/fair-usage-policy.md). Do not invent a common high-speed threshold, reduced speed or reset time.

## What if data does not work in a covered country?

Check the active plan and allowance, installed/enabled Yesim line, selected mobile-data line and the guide's roaming/network settings. Restart as advised by the Help Center, then use [support](support.md). Do not delete the profile as the default fix.

## Does normal coverage include cruise routes and open sea?

[Cruise eSIM guidance](../products/cruise-esim.md) focuses on ports and coastal areas with partner coverage. Connection quality outside the coastal zone is not guaranteed; do not infer a satellite or onboard maritime service.

## Does a compatible device establish coverage everywhere?

No. [Compatibility](compatibility.md), destination inclusion and actual local network coverage require separate checks.
