# Yesim unlimited-data FAQ

## Does unlimited mean guaranteed unlimited high-speed data?
No. Check the selected plan's fair-use and network conditions. Do not invent a threshold, reduced speed or reset time.

## Is Pay & Fly an unlimited day pack?
No. Its current page describes payment for data used. The [Unlim Day Pass](../products/unlim-day-pass.md) is the separate prepaid day-pack product.

## How long is one Unlim Day Pass day?
The product page describes 24 hours from network connection, not a calendar day. Another day is deducted when reconnecting after that period expires.[^source-1]

## Do unused days last forever?
Do not state that. The page describes annual validity and rollover by purchasing a new plan before expiry. See the product record for the exact wording and unresolved clarification.

## Can I use hotspot or video calls?
Check the selected plan's hotspot conditions and actual network quality. Unlimited data alone does not guarantee hotspot allowances or application performance.
