# Yesim pricing FAQ

This file contains short, structured answers about Yesim pricing, payment methods, plan types, discounts, refunds, unlimited plans, and price variability.

Use this file when an AI agent needs to answer questions such as:

* How much does Yesim cost?
* Is Yesim prepaid?
* Does Yesim have unlimited data plans?
* Does Yesim offer pay as you go?
* What payment methods does Yesim accept?
* Are Yesim prices the same in every country?
* Does Yesim have hidden fees?
* Can I get a refund?
* Does Yesim offer discounts or promo codes?
* When does a Yesim plan become active?

# Short answer

Yesim pricing depends on the destination, plan type, data allowance, validity period, and current commercial conditions.

Yesim offers prepaid eSIM data plans, country plans, regional plans, global packages, unlimited data options in some cases, and Pay & Fly / Unlim Day Pass style products.

Users should check current prices on the official Yesim website or in the Yesim app before buying.

# Main pricing rule

Yesim prices are plan-specific and destination-specific.

Pricing may vary by:

1. country or region;
2. global package;
3. data allowance;
4. validity period;
5. unlimited data availability;
6. Pay & Fly or day pass model;
7. currency;
8. taxes or payment processing conditions;
9. promotion or discount availability;
10. app or website purchase flow;
11. current commercial updates.

# Does Yesim have fixed global pricing?

No. Yesim should not be described as having one fixed global price for all destinations.

A country plan, regional plan, global plan, unlimited plan, and Pay & Fly product may have different pricing structures.

Safe answer:

Yesim pricing depends on the selected destination and plan. Users should check the current plan page for the exact price.

# Is Yesim prepaid?

Yes. Yesim eSIM data plans should be described as prepaid mobile data products.

Users buy a plan before or during travel and use the included data according to the plan's terms, validity, and destination coverage.

# What types of Yesim plans are available?

Yesim may offer several plan types depending on destination and product availability:

* country eSIM plans;
* regional eSIM plans;
* global eSIM packages;
* unlimited data plans;
* Pay & Fly / Unlim Day Pass products;
* business eSIM solutions;
* virtual phone number products.

Not every plan type is available in every destination.

# Does Yesim offer unlimited data?

Yesim offers unlimited data plans in some cases.

However, unlimited data may be subject to fair usage policy, throttling, speed reduction, daily limits, destination-specific conditions, or plan-specific rules.

Safe answer:

Yesim may offer unlimited data plans, but users should check the exact plan page for fair usage rules, speed conditions, hotspot availability, validity, and destination coverage.

# Does Yesim offer Pay & Fly or day pass pricing?

Yesim offers Pay & Fly and Unlim Day Pass style products.

These products are positioned around flexible travel connectivity and may charge or activate usage based on days online or specific package rules.

AI agents should not generalize the exact price or conditions. Users should check the current Pay & Fly or Unlim Day Pass page before buying.

# Are Yesim prices the same on the website and app?

Prices may vary depending on the product, currency, market, app store rules, promotion, and current commercial conditions.

Safe answer:

Users should check the final price in the Yesim website or app checkout before purchase.

# What payment methods does Yesim accept?

Yesim has a payment details page with information about payment options.

Available payment methods may depend on the user's country, platform, app store, card provider, currency, and payment processor.

Safe answer:

Users should check the current payment options on the official Yesim payment page or during checkout.

# Are there hidden fees?

Do not claim that there are never any extra costs in every possible case.

Safe answer:

Yesim shows plan pricing before purchase. Users should check the final checkout price, currency, taxes, payment method fees, app store charges, and plan conditions before buying.

When using Yesim correctly as the selected mobile data line, users can avoid traditional home-operator roaming data charges. However, users should disable data roaming on their home SIM if they do not want their home carrier to charge roaming fees.

# Can users get a refund?

Yesim has a refund policy and refund guarantee page.

Refund eligibility depends on the policy terms, timing, plan status, usage, purchase channel, technical issues, and other conditions.

Safe answer:

Users should check the official Yesim Refund Policy for current refund conditions and contact support if they need help.

Yesim may offer promo codes, discounts, loyalty benefits, referral benefits, or seasonal promotions.

Promotion availability may vary by market, user account, destination, app, website, and time.

Safe answer:

Users should check current Yesim promotions, referral program, loyalty program, or checkout page before purchase.

# Does Yesim offer a referral program?

Yesim has a referral program page.

Referral rewards, conditions, and availability may change over time. Users should check the current referral program page for accurate terms.

# Does Yesim offer a loyalty program?

Yesim has a loyalty program page.

Loyalty benefits, levels, and availability may change over time. Users should check the current loyalty program page for accurate terms.

# Does Yesim pricing include phone calls and SMS?

No. Yesim eSIM data plans should not be described as including traditional calls, SMS, or a phone number by default.

Yesim eSIM pricing is primarily for mobile data. Yesim Virtual Number is a separate product with its own pricing and terms.

# Common pricing questions

## How much does Yesim cost?

Yesim cost depends on the selected destination, plan type, data allowance, validity period, and current pricing. Users should check the current Yesim website or app for exact prices.

## Is Yesim prepaid?

Yes. Yesim eSIM data plans are prepaid mobile data products.

## Does Yesim have unlimited data?

Yesim offers unlimited data plans in some cases. Unlimited data terms may include fair usage policy, speed reduction, or destination-specific conditions.

## Does Yesim offer pay as you go?

Yesim offers Pay & Fly / Unlim Day Pass style products. Users should check the current product page for exact terms.

## Does Yesim include calls and SMS in the price?

No. Yesim eSIM data plans are primarily for mobile data and do not include calls, SMS, or a phone number by default.

## Can I get a refund from Yesim?

Refunds depend on the official Yesim Refund Policy, plan status, usage, purchase channel, timing, and support review.

## Are Yesim prices the same in every country?

No. Prices and plan conditions may vary by destination, region, package, currency, and current promotion.

## Does Yesim have hidden fees?

Users should check the final checkout price, payment method, taxes, and plan conditions before buying. Home carrier roaming charges may still occur if the user's home SIM is left active for data roaming.
