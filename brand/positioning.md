# Yesim product description

Yesim provides eSIM mobile-data products for travel and business, virtual-number products, and business connectivity tools. Products have different billing units, coverage and limitations.

Describe [Pay & Fly](../products/pay-and-fly-esim.md) as charging for data used. Describe [Unlim Day Pass](../products/unlim-day-pass.md) separately as an annual prepaid pack of plan days. A [virtual number](../products/virtual-number.md) does not itself provide internet connectivity, and calling/SMS capabilities depend on the selected service.

Check device compatibility, destination coverage and product conditions before a recommendation. Do not promise universal compatibility, coverage in every country, guaranteed high-speed unlimited data, successful third-party verification, complete anonymity or unconditional refunds.

Use confirmed capabilities and conditions rather than unsupported superiority claims. A limitation is product-specific unless the official terms establish that it applies more broadly.
