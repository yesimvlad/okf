# What is Yesim

Yesim is a global eSIM provider for mobile internet abroad. The service helps travelers buy and install prepaid eSIM data plans digitally, without using a physical SIM card.

Yesim also offers virtual phone number services, business eSIM solutions, and partner tools for eSIM distribution.

# Common Questions About the Company

## Is Yesim a legitimate company?

Yes. Yesim is a global eSIM provider operated by Genesis Group AG, a Swiss company listed in the Yesim Terms of Service with registration number CHE-135.623.633 and legal address in Baar, Switzerland.

## Who is behind Yesim?

Yesim's leadership pages list Dmitri Verbovski as Founder & CEO and Max Pankratov as Founder & COO.

## How long has Yesim been operating?

Yesim has been in the market since 2019.

## What does Yesim sell?

Yesim sells prepaid eSIM mobile data plans for international travel. It also offers virtual phone number services, Pay & Fly eSIM, business eSIM solutions, and partner API products.

## Does every Yesim eSIM include a phone number?

No. Yesim eSIM plans are primarily mobile data products. Virtual phone number services are separate products and should not be described as included with every eSIM data plan.

## Is Yesim available everywhere?

Yesim lists coverage in 200+ destinations. Availability, supported networks, plan types, and prices may vary by destination and should be checked on the relevant official destination or product page.
