# Transfer, profile removal and remaining allowance

## Transfer to another phone

First check the new device's exact compatibility. Contact support with complete eSIM information. The official guide describes sending a replacement profile, but explicitly says not all providers support transfer. Do not promise an automatic OS transfer or unlimited reinstallation. [^source-1]

When the replacement is activated, the previous device's plan is deactivated. The same eSIM profile cannot be used on more than one device at a time. This does not prohibit owning separate profiles/products for separate devices. [^source-1]

## Removal and troubleshooting

The no-connection guide advises against removing the Yesim profile or scanning its QR again. The installation guide advises switching the line off after a trip rather than deleting it. Do not treat a previously issued QR as indefinitely reusable. [^source-2] [^source-4]

Use device-specific settings to check the intended data line and roaming, then restart or contact support. The older troubleshooting article includes legacy network steps; do not turn its 3G suggestion into a universal requirement or direct a data-only customer to replace their voice line. [^source-2]

## Remaining allowance and validity

The eSIM section of the Yesim app shows remaining data and days. If the values appear incorrect, send support a screenshot and description. Phone usage counters can help identify consumption but do not replace the purchased plan's validity information. [^source-3]
