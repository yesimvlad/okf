# Yesim English product knowledge reference

English (`en`) is the primary editorial language for this bundle. This record connects the shared product definitions for English answers and localization. Current official policies and the purchased Product Description control any specific condition; English wording does not override a documented local exception.

This is a dated review of public-source facts, not a human product sign-off, app test or live tariff feed. Prices, inventory, destination lists and promotion conditions must be checked at purchase. SEO queries and content hypotheses are kept in a separate editorial scope.

## Short answer

Yesim offers prepaid mobile-data products through eSIM for travel and business. Users need a supported device and a plan covering their trip. Data packages, Pay & Fly and Unlim Day Pass have different billing and validity rules. Virtual Number is a separate product. [^source-1] [^source-2] [^source-3] [^source-4]

## English audience and market boundaries

The English site is https://yesim.app/. Language, customer residence, device sales region and travel destination are separate dimensions. Do not infer US, UK, Canadian or Australian residency, eligibility, currency or number inventory from English copy.

## Choosing a plan

Use this decision sequence with the selected product’s current terms:

1. Collect the complete itinerary and travel duration, including any stop where data is needed.
2. Estimate mobile-data use. Work calls, streaming and tethered devices can change demand.
3. Confirm the exact phone model, regional variant and carrier-unlocked status.
4. Compare eligible country, regional/global, usage-billed and day-pack products.
5. Read the actual package coverage, allowance, billing unit, validity trigger, expiry and hotspot/FUP conditions.
6. Confirm the checkout total, currency, promotion eligibility and purchase channel before paying.

The plan-selection logic above is a synthesis of the linked product requirements, not a personalized price recommendation. [^source-1] [^source-2] [^source-3] [^source-6] [^source-9] [^source-10] [^source-12] [^source-13] [^source-14]

## Buying, installing and using the profile

The published flow is to select a destination/product, complete payment and follow installation instructions. On Apple devices the current guide includes app, QR and manual methods. Keep an internet connection available and follow the exact device instructions. [^source-1] [^source-8]

Select the intended line for mobile data and follow its Data Roaming settings. Check home-SIM roaming and automatic data switching separately. Do not delete the profile as a default troubleshooting step; the Apple guide advises switching it off after a trip. [^source-8]

## Questions and answers

### What is Yesim?

Yesim offers prepaid eSIM mobile-data products for travel and business. Choose a compatible device and a plan covering the trip. Virtual Number is a separate product. [^source-1] [^source-4]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/esim.md)

### Does the English website mean Yesim is only for US or UK customers?

No. English is a language version. Establish residence, destination and purchase eligibility separately; do not infer a country-specific offer from the language. [^source-1] [^source-5]

[Detailed knowledge](../markets.md)

### Does a Yesim data-only plan include a phone number, calls or SMS?

No. A data-only plan supplies mobile data. A separate number product has its own capabilities. Internet-based app calls use a different mechanism from cellular calls. [^source-2] [^source-4]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/calls-sms.md)

### How do I know whether my phone supports Yesim?

Check the exact model, regional version, eSIM support and carrier-unlocked status on the compatibility page. A series name or newer-generation rule is insufficient. [^source-6]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/compatibility.md)

### Does Dual SIM or an EID prove compatibility?

Dual SIM does not necessarily mean eSIM. An EID is a useful indicator, but still check the exact device and operator lock. [^source-6]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/compatibility.md)

### Can I assume every China-sold or newer iPhone works?

No blanket sales-region or generation rule is safe. Confirm the exact regional model and current device guidance; resolve conflicting lists before purchase. [^source-6]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/compatibility.md)

### Which Yesim product should I choose for a trip?

Start with the full itinerary, duration, data demand and phone compatibility. Compare fixed-data, regional/global, Pay & Fly and day-pack options using the selected product’s current conditions. [^source-1] [^source-2] [^source-3] [^source-13] [^source-14]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/pricing.md)

### Will one Europe or global package cover every country I visit?

Verify each itinerary country against that exact package. The region name, brand-wide coverage and a country landing page do not establish inclusion in the purchased plan. [^source-13] [^source-14]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/coverage.md)

### How do I install a Yesim eSIM on iPhone?

Use the current Yesim app/device instructions and an internet connection. The Apple guide provides app, QR and manual installation routes; screen labels vary by software version. [^source-8]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/activation.md)

### Is installing the profile the same as starting the data plan?

Installation adds the profile. The selected plan defines its validity trigger. Treat purchase, installation and first network connection as separate events. [^source-7] [^source-8]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/activation.md)

### When does my plan’s validity begin?

Check the purchased plan description. The Help Center lists activation after purchase, installation, first compatible-network connection, or installation plus suitable network connection. [^source-7]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/activation.md)

### Can I install before departure without starting every plan?

Do not assume that. Check the plan’s trigger before installing. Pre-installation instructions for one product are not a universal validity rule. [^source-7] [^source-8]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/activation.md)

### How should I set the data line and roaming?

Follow the installation guide for the Yesim line. Review home-SIM data roaming and automatic data switching separately; retaining a home SIM does not guarantee zero home-operator charges. [^source-8]

[Detailed knowledge](../concepts/roaming.md)

### Is Pay & Fly a daily unlimited plan?

Its payment section describes charges for data used. Destination rates vary. It is not the same billing model as Unlim Day Pass, despite unlimited wording in the page title. [^source-2] [^source-3]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/pay-and-fly-esim.md)

### Can I top up Pay & Fly?

The reviewed page advertises manual top-up, optional auto top-up and a non-expiring balance. Verify the current rate, balance and account settings for the trip. [^source-2]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/pay-and-fly-esim.md)

### What is Unlim Day Pass?

An annual prepaid pack of unlimited-data usage days. Its own supported-destination list, validity and day-consumption rules apply. [^source-3]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/unlim-day-pass.md)

### Does a plan day end at midnight?

The product FAQ describes a 24-hour period starting on network connection. A further day is deducted when reconnecting after the previous period ends. [^source-3]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/unlim-day-pass.md)

### Do unused Unlim Day Pass days remain valid forever?

Do not promise unconditional lifetime validity. The page specifies annual validity and rollover when another plan is purchased before expiry; confirm the actual package conditions. [^source-3]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/unlim-day-pass.md)

### Does unlimited data always mean unlimited full-speed data?

No. Partner networks may apply fair-use limits and reduce speed. Check the selected terms; do not invent a common quota, throttled speed or reset time. [^source-10]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/unlimited-data.md)

### Can I use Personal Hotspot or tether another device?

The Help Center says tethering is available on most plans and depends on the local operator. Shared usage consumes plan data; availability in every situation is not guaranteed. [^source-9]

[Detailed knowledge](../concepts/hotspot.md)

### Is 5G or a minimum speed guaranteed everywhere?

No. The destination pages describe network generations as dependent on the provider and location. Device support, plan conditions and actual network availability must be checked separately. [^source-19]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/coverage.md)

### What does a Yesim Virtual Number provide?

A separate number service with the functions specified for the purchased product. Check rental, included capabilities and renewal; it does not supply mobile data by itself. [^source-4] [^source-5]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/virtual-number.md)

### Will a virtual number always receive a WhatsApp, Telegram or bank code?

No. Third-party acceptance and OTP delivery are not guaranteed. Check the selected service and the platform’s rules. [^source-5]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/virtual-number.md)

### Can I make outgoing calls with any virtual number?

Only if the purchased service includes outgoing calls. OTP-only functionality is for verification-related inbound communications. [^source-5]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/virtual-number.md)

### Can I use an existing messenger account over Yesim data?

Internet-based applications use the data connection and their own accounts. Account verification is a separate question; data access alone does not establish number acceptance. [^source-19] [^source-5]

[Detailed knowledge](../faq/calls-sms.md)

### Can I keep a virtual number indefinitely?

Check rental expiry and renewal. Do not promise indefinite ownership or recovery of an expired number. [^source-5]

[Detailed knowledge](../products/virtual-number.md)

### Can Yesim products be used to call emergency services?

The Terms exclude emergency calling/messaging through Yesim products. Use a service capable of reaching local emergency services. [^source-5]

[Detailed knowledge](../policies/limitations.md)

### Where do I find current prices, currencies and discounts?

## Source conflicts and uncertainty handling

Trial and cruise marketing include broad refund guarantees; the current Refund Policy defines actual eligibility. Business-number marketing includes no-KYC/anonymity wording; the Terms permit verification requests and the Privacy Policy describes data processing. Do not turn these marketing statements into unconditional product promises. [^source-11] [^source-17] [^source-18] [^source-5]


The retrieved English Pay & Fly title still says unlimited, while its payment section says charges depend on data used. The reviewed billing section determines the usage explanation. [^source-2]

Unlim Day Pass page sections contain inconsistent destination counts and pack sizes. Do not fix these in an answer by guessing; use the actual selectable product and current destination list. Annual validity and conditional renewal must accompany any statement about remaining days. [^source-3]

Device lists can contain broad sales-region wording. Check the exact current model rather than treating such language as a permanent rule for an entire country or future device family. [^source-6]

If a required price, activation trigger, phone variant or purchased number function cannot be established, request that detail or mark it unresolved. Do not fabricate a value to complete an answer.

## Common answer failures to avoid

- Applying day-pack billing to Pay & Fly.
- Treating every newer phone or every Dual SIM device as compatible.
- Assuming every plan starts only after landing.
- Quoting a global coverage total as an individual package’s coverage.
- Promising maximum speed, unlimited hotspot or one common FUP threshold.
- Saying that every eSIM includes a number or every virtual number supports all calls/SMS.
- Guaranteeing third-party verification, permanent number retention or refunds after installation.
- Guessing a local-currency price, universal promotion or resident-only offer from language.
