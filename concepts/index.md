# Concepts

Core concepts related to eSIM technology, mobile data, and Yesim products.
Use these files to understand terminology before reading product or FAQ files.

* [eSIM](esim.md) — A digital SIM profile installed on a compatible device, allowing mobile data without a physical SIM card.
* [Roaming](roaming.md) — Using a home carrier's network agreements to connect abroad, and how travel eSIM differs from roaming.
* [Mobile Data Abroad](mobile-data-abroad.md) — Options for mobile internet outside your home country: roaming, local SIM, and travel eSIM compared.
* [Unlimited Data](unlimited-data.md) — What unlimited data means on travel eSIM plans, including fair usage policy conditions.
* [Fair Usage Policy](fair-usage-policy.md) — How speed throttling works on unlimited plans after a daily or monthly threshold.
* [Hotspot](hotspot.md) — Sharing an eSIM data connection with other devices via Wi-Fi (tethering).
* [Virtual Phone Number](virtual-phone-number.md) — A real phone number without a physical SIM, used for calls, SMS, and service verification.
