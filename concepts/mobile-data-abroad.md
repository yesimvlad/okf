# What is Mobile Data Abroad

Mobile data abroad means using a mobile internet connection in a country other than your home country. When a device leaves its home network coverage area, it needs a way to connect to a local mobile network at the destination.

There are three main options for mobile data abroad:

1. **Roaming** — using the home carrier's network agreements to connect abroad. Usually the most expensive option.
2. **Local SIM card** — buying a physical SIM card from a local carrier at the destination. Requires a SIM card slot and in-person purchase.
3. **Travel eSIM** — installing a prepaid digital SIM profile before or after arrival. No physical card, instant activation.

# Why Travelers Choose Travel eSIM

- No need to find a local carrier store on arrival
- Plan can be purchased and installed before the trip
- One eSIM can cover multiple countries on regional plans
- Home SIM stays active for calls and SMS (on dual SIM devices)
- Prepaid — no surprise bills after the trip

# How Travel eSIM Works Abroad

1. The user purchases a travel eSIM plan for the destination.
2. The eSIM profile is installed on the device before or after arrival.
3. On arrival, the device connects to a local network through the eSIM provider's agreements.
4. The user pays the eSIM provider's rate — the home carrier is not involved.
5. Data is used from the prepaid plan until it runs out or the validity period ends.

This is not roaming in the traditional sense. The home carrier does not charge roaming fees. See [Roaming](roaming.md) for a full explanation.

# Common Questions

## What is the cheapest way to get mobile data abroad?

Travel eSIM and local SIM cards are generally cheaper than roaming through a home carrier. Travel eSIM has the advantage of no physical card and pre-trip activation. Exact pricing depends on the destination and plan. Check current plan pages before purchase.

## Can I use my phone abroad without paying roaming charges?

Yes, if you use a travel eSIM or local SIM card instead of relying on your home carrier's roaming service. With a travel eSIM, your home carrier is not involved in the data connection.

## Do I need to turn off roaming when using a travel eSIM?

On dual SIM devices, it is recommended to disable data roaming on the physical SIM to avoid accidental roaming charges, and set the eSIM as the active data line for the trip.

## Will my home number still work abroad if I use a travel eSIM?

On dual SIM devices, yes. The physical SIM stays active for calls and SMS from the home number. The eSIM handles mobile data. Both work simultaneously.

## Can I use a travel eSIM in multiple countries?

Yes, with a regional or global plan. Country-specific plans only cover the designated country. Check the plan coverage before purchase.
