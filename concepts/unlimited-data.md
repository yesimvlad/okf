# What is Unlimited Data

An unlimited data plan provides mobile internet access without a fixed data volume limit. The user does not need to track how many gigabytes they have used or purchase top-ups when data runs out.

On travel eSIM plans, unlimited data is not the same as uncapped high-speed data. Unlimited plans include a fair usage policy (FUP) that allows the provider to reduce speeds after a daily or monthly threshold is reached. The connection remains active — only the speed is affected.

# What "Unlimited" Means on Travel eSIM Plans

| Claim | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Unlimited data | No fixed GB cap — connection stays active throughout the plan period |
| Subject to FUP | Speeds may be reduced after a daily threshold |
| High-speed data | Full speeds apply up to the FUP threshold |
| Reduced speeds after threshold | Connection active, speeds typically 1–3 Mbps |
| Hotspot included | Sharing allowed, may have separate FUP threshold |

Always check the specific plan page for exact FUP conditions before purchase.

# Unlimited Data vs Fixed Volume Plans

| | Unlimited Plan | Fixed Volume Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Data limit | No GB cap | Fixed GB (e.g. 1GB, 5GB, 10GB) |
| Speed after threshold | Reduced (FUP) | Data stops (cap reached) |
| Best for | Heavy users, long trips, video calls | Light users, short trips |
| Predictability | Fixed daily/monthly cost | Fixed data amount |
| Top-up needed | No | Yes, if data runs out |

# When Unlimited Plans Make Sense

- Heavy data users — frequent video calls, streaming, hotspot use
- Long trips where tracking data usage is inconvenient
- Business travelers who need reliable connectivity throughout the day
- Users who share data with travel companions via hotspot
- Users who want a fixed daily cost without monitoring usage

# When Fixed Volume Plans May Be Better

- Short trips with light data usage (maps, messaging, email)
- Users who primarily use Wi-Fi and need mobile data as backup
- Destinations where unlimited plans are not available
- Budget travelers who want the lowest possible cost per trip

# Common Questions

## Is unlimited data truly unlimited on Yesim?

Yesim unlimited plans have no fixed GB cap — the connection stays active for the full plan period. However, speeds may be reduced after a daily FUP threshold is reached. After the threshold, the connection remains active at reduced speeds. Full speeds resume the next day. See [Fair usage policy](fair-usage-policy.md).

## What speeds can I expect on an unlimited Yesim plan?

Full speeds apply up to the FUP threshold. After the threshold, speeds are typically sufficient for messaging, maps, and light browsing but may not support HD video streaming. Exact speeds depend on the local network and destination.

## Can I use hotspot on Yesim unlimited plans?

Yesim supports hotspot on most unlimited plans. Hotspot data may be subject to a separate FUP threshold. Check the specific plan details before purchase. See [Hotspot](hotspot.md).

## Does Yesim unlimited work in every country?

Unlimited plans are available for specific countries and regions. Not all destinations have unlimited plan options. Check the destination page on the Yesim app or website for available plan types.

## Can I pause an unlimited plan between uses?

Yesim's Pay & Fly and Unlim Day Pass products allow the user to activate individual days from a purchased pack. Days not yet activated do not expire immediately — check the specific product page for validity terms.
