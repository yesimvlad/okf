# Unlimited mobile data

Unlimited data and unlimited speed are different properties. The relevant plan may apply fair-use or network-management conditions; the term alone does not establish guaranteed high-speed service.

For Yesim, check the selected unlimited plan's Product Description. [Unlim Day Pass](../products/unlim-day-pass.md) is a day-based product. [Pay & Fly](../products/pay-and-fly-esim.md) is currently described as charging for data used and must not be classified as a daily unlimited product.

Do not invent a high-speed allowance, reduced speed, midnight reset, daily/monthly reset rule or separate hotspot allowance. These must be documented for the exact plan. Network congestion and coverage can also affect speed independently of fair-use limits.
