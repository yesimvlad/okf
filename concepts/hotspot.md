# Hotspot and tethering

The Help Center describes tethering as available on most plans, dependent on the local operator. Shared usage consumes plan data. It is not guaranteed in every situation or exempt from fair-use conditions.

Check the selected product, local network and device settings before relying on tethering for work or multiple devices. Do not invent a separate hotspot quota, unlimited allowance or minimum speed.
