# Hotspot and tethering

Hotspot shares a device's mobile-data connection with other devices. The selected plan's tethering permission, allowance, fair-use treatment and actual device/network conditions must be checked separately.

The current [Pay & Fly page](../products/pay-and-fly-esim.md) advertises hotspot mode. This does not establish unlimited tethering or an identical allowance on every Yesim product.

Do not invent a separate hotspot quota or say that tethering is exempt from fair-use rules. For working, video calls or multiple connected devices, assess actual data demand and the selected terms.
