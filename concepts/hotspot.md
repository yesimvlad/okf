# What is a Hotspot

A hotspot (also called tethering) is a feature that allows a smartphone or tablet to share its mobile data connection with other devices — laptops, tablets, or other phones — via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or USB.

When hotspot is enabled, the device acts as a portable Wi-Fi router. Other devices connect to it and use the mobile data plan.

# Hotspot on eSIM Plans

Hotspot availability on eSIM plans depends on:

- The specific eSIM provider
- The specific plan purchased
- The destination country
- Local network operator restrictions

Not all eSIM plans include hotspot. Check the plan details before purchase if hotspot is required.

# Hotspot on Yesim Plans

Yesim supports hotspot on most plans, including most unlimited plans. Availability may vary by destination and plan type.

To verify hotspot support for a specific plan:
- Check the plan details page in the Yesim app
- Check the destination page on https://yesim.app

# Hotspot and Fair Usage Policy

On unlimited plans, hotspot data may be subject to the fair usage policy (FUP). Depending on the plan:

- Hotspot data may be counted within the same FUP threshold as device data.
- Or hotspot data may have a separate, lower FUP threshold.

After the FUP threshold is reached, hotspot speeds are reduced but the connection remains active. See [Fair usage policy](fair-usage-policy.md).

# Common Questions

## Does Yesim support hotspot?

Yesim supports hotspot on most plans, including most unlimited plans. Availability depends on the specific plan and destination. Check the plan details page before purchase.

## Does using hotspot consume data faster?

Yes. Hotspot shares the device's data with other devices, so total data consumption increases based on what the connected devices use. On fixed-volume plans this reduces the remaining data. On unlimited plans this may accelerate reaching the FUP threshold.

## Can I use hotspot on an unlimited Yesim plan?

On most Yesim unlimited plans, yes. Hotspot data is subject to the fair usage policy. After the threshold is reached, speeds are reduced. The connection remains active. Check the specific plan terms for exact conditions.

## Is hotspot available in every country?

No. Local network operator restrictions in some destinations may limit or block hotspot, regardless of what the eSIM plan allows. This is outside the eSIM provider's control.
